Metric contracts

Paid revenue: sum of captured payments by payment date, after refunds are handled separately. Invoices are not recognised as paid revenue.

Conversion rate: leads created in selected period who later paid, divided by leads created in period. An immature cohort is labelled provisional; for a production system, use a maturity window or fixed 30-day conversion.

Discovery show rate: discovery calls attended / discovery calls booked, on the same lead cohort.

CPQL: ad spend / qualified leads. Requires UTM attribution and a consistent qualification definition.

Chapter readiness: progress through reading, recall notes, question practice (≥70%) and timed past-paper proficiency (≥80%). Demonstration rubric; to be validated with advisors and students.

Quality: optional student feedback; no feedback is not a negative score. Show sample counts to avoid false precision.

Weekly meaningful coverage (candidate-chosen direction): distinct eligible enrolled students completing at least one qualifying, evidence-backed action this week / all eligible enrolled students, including inactive students. Proposed qualifying events: submitted quiz attempt, completed practice with recorded attempt, evidence-linked planned study task, or recorded chapter readiness evidence; exclude opening Home, coins, Realm play and timer starts alone.

Week-on-week return: prior-week meaningful learners still eligible this week who also performed a meaningful action this week / prior-week meaningful learners still eligible this week. Empty prior cohorts show a dash, not 0%.

Product guardrail: measure comprehension with separate assessment; keep optional student burden feedback and advisor effort visible. Proposed definitions, subject to candidate and stakeholder validation.

Practice guardrails: don't optimise app screen time alone, don't use wearable recovery data for ranking, and do not store student-sensitive records in client-side demo code.
